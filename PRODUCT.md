# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Knowledge workers, students, writers, and developers who seek mindful focus without the harsh pressure, abrasive alarms, or gamified stress of conventional productivity apps.

## Product Purpose

Garden Timer transforms the Pomodoro focus technique into a serene botanical ritual. By tying focused work sessions to the organic growth of plants, it provides gentle positive reinforcement: deep work nurtures a garden of blooming trees, while premature skips cause plants to wilt.

## Positioning

Unlike sterile countdown timers or punishing streak apps, Garden Timer anchors productivity in living natural metaphors. Generative algorithmic botanical growth and real-time celestial lighting (sun and moon cycles) create a quiet, grounding companion for deep work.

## Operating Context

Ambient web desktop and laptop workstations; pinned or positioned beside active writing, coding, or study windows. Users glance occasionally at visual progress rather than watching numbers tick down.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Focus durations customizable from 1 to 120 minutes (default: 25 min).
- Standard Pomodoro intervals: Work sessions followed by short breaks (5 min) and long breaks (15 min after every 4 sessions).
- Procedural organic canvas tree renderer with multiple biological stages: seed, sprout, sapling, full bloom with seasonal pink petals.
- Real-time celestial day/night sky with dynamic sun and moon arcs synchronized with local time.
- Gentle consequence mechanic: completing a session advances plant growth; skipping wilts the active plant.
- Multi-session garden view tracking completed blooms and total focus sessions.

## Brand Commitments

- Calming organic palette: warm cream ground (`#f4f1e8`), ink typography (`#2b2b2b`), botanical sage/forest green (`#4f7d46`), terracotta danger (`#b5654a`).
- Editorial serif headings paired with clean system sans-serif and tabular mono numerals.
- Warm, grounded, serene, non-intrusive voice. No artificial urgency or guilt-inducing gamification.

## Evidence on Hand

- Fully functioning React + HTML5 Canvas web application in `src/`.
- Procedural generative plant algorithm in `src/canvas/drawPlant.js`.
- Pomodoro cycle state machine and settings in `src/state/` and `src/hooks/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Growth Over Pressure:** Celebrate time invested with living beauty rather than guilt over lost minutes.
2. **Ambient Serenity:** The timer is a calm background companion, not a demanding distraction.
3. **Honest Consequence with Kindness:** Skipped sessions wilt the plant gently, reinforcing intention without harsh punishment.
4. **Natural Rhythm:** Sync with daylight and natural cycles to ground digital work in real time.
